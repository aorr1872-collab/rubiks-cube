    Controls

    Use the mouse, the keyboard, or both.

    Left-drag on the cubeTurn the layer under the pointer, in the direction you drag
    Hold both buttons + dragSpin the whole cube to look around
    Scroll wheelZoom in and out
    U D L R F BTurn that face clockwise
    Shift + faceTurn anticlockwise (prime)
    2 then RInner layer: the 2nd layer from the right
    W then RWide turn: outer two layers (Rw)
    3 W ROuter three layers (3Rw)
    M E SMiddle slices (3×3 and 5×5)
    X Y ZRotate the whole cube (does not start the timer)
    SpaceScramble
    Ctrl + ZUndo last move
    EscReset to solved

    The timer starts on your first turn after scrambling and stops when the cube is solved. On phones, use the move buttons below the cube and drag with a finger to look around.
